Here's my Dublin recommendations. Most of these are around where I spent my time in college. If I share this with you and you just go to Temple bar, you're an op. 

***

#### Cafés ☕

Shoe Lane – small but pleasant good vibes café, weird seating

Cornucopia – poshy vibes but lovely, food looks really good but is stupid expensive

Café Tri Via – small cute and nice, soup and sandwich is cheap for lunch

La Pausa Café – out the way, but I was here once and it was nice

Zanzibar Locke – nice place you can work with your laptop for a long time

Dolce Sicily – my favourite place for a coffee and cake to celebrate on rare occasions

Il Caffee de Napoli – all of the aesthetic aura farmers sit outside of this cafe, does look cool though to be fair

Books Upstairs – nice bookstore, café upstairs

Laudrée – yummy rich French hot chocolate

***

#### Things to Do 🧗‍♀️

Howth Cliff Walk – nice hike, doable whenever and easily accessible, also you’re in Howth which is nice

Bray/Greystones Hike – really nice but a bit far, doable though, Bray is lovely so end there and get a pint or some scampi

Forty Foot – lots of people go here for sea swimming but I have never been

Sandymount Beach – nice beach, nice walks

Howth Beach – nice beach, also you’re in Howth which is nice

Trinity College Dublin – worth a visit to be fair, sit on college green at the back for a recharge, could probably skip the book of kells

Dublin Castle – haven’t been in ages, it is cool though to be fair

St Patrick’s Cathedral – haven’t been, but looks cool from the outside

Guinness Storehouse – have never been, but apparently it’s actually quite good

Stephen’s Green – nice in theory, always crowded and seagulls are on crack

Merrion Square – nice and not crowded like Stephen’s Green

National Gallery – free, yeah

EPIC Museum – haven’t been, apparently quite good though

National History Museum – free, actually class istg, go look at the long ass boat

Collin’s Barracks – oddly a lot of great events here all the time, I think there’s a military museum in there too

Phoenix Park – big ass park, zoo is there, light show on at Christmas

Iveagh Gardens – nice for a walk if you’re in the area

Hodges Figgis – nice bookstore near Trinity

Books Upstairs – nice bookstore, café upstairs

Secret Bookstore – my favourite bookstore, goated, records too

Lexicon Library and Cultural Centre, Dun Laoghaire – just a lovely public library, could make a day out of it if you do other things in Dun Laoghaire

Board and Brewed, Dun Laoghaire – cute little board game shop, could make a day out of it if you do other things in Dun Laoghaire

Harbour, Dun Laoghaire – a nice harbour stroll, seafood vans along the way, could make a day out of it if you do other things in Dun Laoghaire

Viking Theatre, Clontarf – nice small theatre, could go for a walk along the coast road and get posh dinner in Clontarf

Saint Anne’s Park, Clontarf/Raheny – nice park but out the way, I think you can get the bus somewhere

Savoy – cheap cinema in the middle of town

Light House – great cinema, trendy, always show great stuff

Irish Film Institute – haven’t been, meant to be good and they seem to show cool things here all the time

Stella, Rathmines – fancy cinema with nice seats, apparently

George’s Arcade – permanent market, quite nice

The Hideout – pool tables, byob

Drunken Fish – karaoke, solid vibes, korean food is also meant to be banging

Token – arcade, what you would expect

Murphy’s Ice Cream – free samples, and they have always been good to be fair

#### Areas 🌍

Around Trinity – this is where I would spend my time (and most people do) if I wanted to see the nice parts in Dublin

Howth – nice seaside area, good restaurants and seafood, hike, seals, pints, locals

Skerries – my favorite place in Dublin, quite quiet, lovely beach, chill, cosy pubs

Smithfield – lots of cultural stuff to do

Liberties – gentrified working class area, nice stuff to do but not loads, historical

Temple Bar – touristy, you should walk through to be fair, but don’t waste your time or money here, there’s much better places to do that
