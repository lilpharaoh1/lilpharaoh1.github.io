Most of these I can back from my own experience, while others are recommendations from people I think would have a good opinion. If they’re good, your welcome. It not, it was someone else.

***

#### Cafés ☕

Cult Coffee – specialty coffee on a nice street

Origin Coffee – specialty coffee, good for eavesdropping

La Barantine – no seating, coffee mid, but pastries are honestly goated, weird opening hours

Not Just Coffee – good treats but more expensive then they’re worth tbh, dotted around town

The Milkman – big line but never let me down with genuinely banging pastries and cakes

Margot – Fancy shmancy café restaurant but cakes look good

Books n’ Cup – open till late, nice vibe

***

#### Food 🍜

Freddy’s – touristy but honestly great and lowk cheap

The Green Mantle – mid pub but the burgers were surprisingly good

Old Bell Inn – good food pub, expensive

Teutcher’s – great food pub and with nice vibe, expensive

Kalpna – great vegetarian buffet lunch, dinner too expensive to be worth it

Tanjore – authentic South Indian food, get the thali

Taco Libre – haven’t been but supposed to be very good and authentic

El Dorado Taqueria – authentic Mexican food + tamales

Noodles Home – busy but really good

Noodles and Dumplings – really good

Dumplings of China – really good I’ve heard

津宴 Amazing – really good, huge portions, cheap

Satoru – moderately good, huge portions, cheap

Xiangbala Hotpot – recommended to me as best for hot pot

Nanyang Malaysian Restaurant – Huge portions of good Thai food

Hoi An – banging vietnamese

Pomelo – recommended to me as extremely good fusion, long waiting list

Starter Culture – new, haven’t been, looks great though

Moratti – best doorty kebab, huge, cheap, pizzas are meant to be good too

Nile Valley Café – cheap big wraps that are actually decent

Mosque Kitchen – cheap good cafeteria type indian, iykyk

Bánh Mì Brothers – cheap bánh mìs for lunch

***

#### Pubs + Bars 🍻

The Ventoux – cool rustic bar, near the meadows

Brauhaus – my favourite bar, cool wine and beer selection, rude staff though

Seamus’ – genuinely good Irish pub, near the meadows

Earl of Marchmont – youthful local pub, good character

Royal Oak – small pub teetering on old man-y, good tunes though

Captains Bar – bit touristy but can be relied upon for mid touristy live music

Cabaret Voltaire – haven’t been but some locals go so idk

Sandy Bell’s – maybe worst (Irish) pub I’ve been to, touristy Irish music, staff are genuinely rude and often horrible people, worth going if you want an unironic bad experience

Powder Room – nice little pub with live music on a Thursday

Southsider – extremely cheap local bar with students, you’re getting what you pay for

Dagda – my favourite pub, lovely and cosy, small though

Decanter – lovely wine + picky bits bar, in lovely part of town too

Margot – I believe this turns into a wine bar in the evening

False Widow – really love this pub, cool romantic rustic, very very small though

Joker and the Thief – nice pub on Leith walk

Cloister’s – english-y pub but lots of good beers actually, supposedly they have a good lunch and pint deal

Pear Tree – mid and expensive, but huge beer garden in city centre

Summerhall – nicer beer garden, near the Meadows

***

#### Things to Do 🧗‍♀️

Portobello – Seaside neighbourhood with padel courts and loads of lovely cafes

Pentlands – Quick Bus to a solid hike, bus to Flotterstone Inn, then walk to Currie, shorter and longer hike options, high recommend if you have a (half-)day of time, plan rough bus schedule ahead of time

Queensferry – busable nearby town, actually lovely and cute but also small so don’t expect much

Blackford Park – nice walk if you’re in the area

The Meadows – sit out and chill if it’s nice outside, there’s also a hippy co-op there with a small cafe, always stuff on to people watch, this will be you’re outside resting area

Arthur’s Seat – hike, takes maybe one hour, in the city

Botanical Gardens – Good not Great, HUGE houses nearby to have a look at

Surgeons Hall Museum – worth going and free for students I think, kind of gross but yeah cool

National Gallery – free, yeah

Fudge Kitchen – delicious fudge, free samples

Alien Bloc/Roc – haven’t been, climbing gym

Roxy Lanes – haven’t been, bowling and pool

Armchair Books – best bookstore in Edinburgh probably, very cool and cute

Tills Bookshop – cute bookstore, expensive though

Armstrongs – second hand vintage clothing shops dotted around the city, good and fairly cheap, cool interiors for the most part

Black Lion Games – cute little board game shop if you’re nearby

Ancient Robot Gamers – good board game shop in Leith, recommend if you have a free evening

Bedlam Theatre – small nice theatre with a cute bar inside

Cameo – cool cinema w/ cheap tickets, bar and café inside, renovated

Filmhouse – cool cinema w/ cheap tickets

Sol Sauna (Granton) – nice sauna experience, far out but there’s a market thing nearby for food and pints and coffee and bits

***

#### Areas

Grassmarket – touristy but it's just a great spot, good Saturday market

Stockbridge – fancy area with nice places and lots of charity shops, expensive

Morningside – fancy area with nice places and lots of charity shops, expensive

Leith – nice, working class but gentrified, like Phibsborough

Bruntsfield – my favourite, near the Meadows, nice cafés and posh pubs
